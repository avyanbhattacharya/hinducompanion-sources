# Legal notes for hinducompanion.com

> **Not legal advice.** The operator is US-based; consult an IP lawyer
> before investing heavily. This is a risk map, not counsel.

## Copyright (US operator)

- US term: **life + 70 years** (India is life + 60 — use the stricter US
  rule). Works **published before 1930** are public domain outright.
- **DMCA** is the practical exposure: US hosts act on takedown notices
  quickly, and music labels file them aggressively. Register a DMCA agent
  with the Copyright Office at launch to keep safe-harbor protection.
- Never copy from: modern translations, lyrics sites, DrikPanchang-style
  pages, sanskritdocuments.org (see `sources/sanskrit.md`).

## Religious-sensitivity law

- **BNS Section 299** (replaced IPC 295A, July 2024) punishes *deliberate
  and malicious* acts intended to outrage religious feelings — up to 3
  years. Courts require proven malicious intent; a sincere companion site
  with an honest error doesn't meet that bar.
- The US has no blasphemy law, so criminal exposure from the US is minimal.
- **The real threat model is not prosecution — it's backlash**: mass
  reporting, app-store removal, payment-processor complaints, domain
  pressure. These work across borders without any courtroom.

## Launch checklist

1. **Disclaimers**: informational purpose only, not priestly guidance;
   panchang method disclosed (Lahiri ayanamsa; regional variations exist).
2. **Sourcing**: cite the text/sampradaya for practices; never present one
   tradition's observance as universal.
3. **Correction mechanism**: visible "report an error" path; fix flagged
   errors fast — speed of correction is the best outrage insurance.
4. **No user comments at launch**: UGC on religious topics is a moderation
   nightmare and triggers intermediary-liability duties.
5. **Terms of Service**: US governing law, but moderate content to Indian
   audience sensibilities anyway.
6. **DMCA agent** registered; repeat-infringer policy in Terms.
